How the numbers are calculated

Shopify fee. The percentage is charged on the order total — item, shipping and sales tax — and the 30¢ fixed part is charged once per order. International cards add 1%; premium cards are blended by the share you enter.

fee = (total × (std + intl×share + premium×share)) + 0.30

Break-even ROAS. The ROAS at which ad profit is exactly zero. It depends on your margin, not your category, so it is different for every store.

BEP ROAS = revenue ÷ (revenue − fees − COGS − shipping)

POAS. Profit per ad dollar — (revenue − fees − COGS − shipping) ÷ ad spend. Above 1.00 the ads paid for themselves; below 1.00 they bought revenue at a loss.

Why ROAS flatters you. A 3.0× ROAS on a 25% margin store is break-even at best. The platform reports revenue, not profit.

Refunds. Shopify keeps the card fee when you refund, so a refunded month shows a higher effective fee rate. This calculator models fees on the gross order, the way Shopify charges them.
